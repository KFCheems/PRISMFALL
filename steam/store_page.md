## English

**Title:** PRISMFALL
**Tagline:** Bend light. Mix color. Survive the fall.

### Short description (≤300 chars)

> A neon roguelite survival shooter about bending light. Your weapons fire on their own — you dodge, dash and build. Split your arsenal into red, green and blue, fuse max-level weapons into Solar, Glacial and Void, and detonate a screen-wide Prism Burst.

### About this game

You are a fragment of living light, falling through the four layers of a dying spectrum. Everything down here wants to put you out.

**PRISMFALL** is a fast, gorgeous *bullet heaven*: your weapons fire automatically while you weave through hundreds of geometric enemies, scoop up photons, and turn every level-up into a sharper, brighter, more ridiculous build. Ten minutes. One boss. One more run.

**Mix light, not just stats**
- Every weapon and passive belongs to a hue: **Crimson** for raw force, **Verdant** for life and reach, **Azure** for tempo.
- Max out two weapons of different hues and **fuse** them: **Solar** burns and chains lightning, **Glacial** freezes and shatters, **Void** homes, heals and tears open singularities.
- Hold all three fusions at once to reach **Prismatic Resonance** — pure white light, every effect at once.

**Play it your way**
- **15 weapons** — crescent slashes, orbiting blades, lobbed mortars, piercing lasers, whips, turrets, drones, boomerang prisms and more.
- **15 passives** and **Resonance** bonuses that reward committing to a color.
- **Prism Burst**: charge your Overdrive with kills, then unleash a screen-wide wave tinted by your dominant hue — damage, healing or a total freeze.
- A **dash** with invincibility frames, because sometimes the answer is *through*.

**Fall deeper**
- **4 hand-crafted stages** — the Nebula Shallows, the Crimson Expanse, the Verdant Lattice and the Singularity — each with its own enemies, events and music.
- **4 multi-pattern bosses**, from the Hexwarden to the Null Prism at the bottom of the spectrum.
- **6 characters** with unique starting weapons and playstyles.
- **Ascension** levels for every stage and an **Endless** mode for the brave.
- Spend Lumens in the **Prism Forge** to grow stronger between runs; unlock characters, weapons and achievements.

**Built to feel great**
- Thousands of glowing shapes at a smooth framerate, HDR bloom, hitstop and screen shake (all adjustable).
- Fully synthesized, reactive soundtrack that rises with the chaos.
- **Full controller support**, Steam Deck friendly, keyboard & mouse with optional manual aiming.
- Accessibility options: reduced flashing, screen-shake strength, damage numbers, render scale.
- English and Simplified Chinese.

### Controller support

**Full controller support** (Xbox, PlayStation, generic XInput). Move with the left stick, aim with the right stick (manual aim) or let auto-aim handle it, **A / RB / RT** to dash, **Y / LB / LT** for Prism Burst, **Start** to pause. All menus are navigable with the D-pad and stick.

### Steam Deck

Designed to run on Steam Deck: 16:9 UI scales cleanly to 1280×800, all menus work with the built-in controls, no keyboard input or launcher is required, and text is readable at Deck resolution. For best battery life use *Render scale 75%* and *Bloom 0.75* in Settings → Video.

## 简体中文

**名称：** PRISMFALL（棱光坠落）
**标语：** 折射光芒，调和色彩，在坠落中存活。

### 简短描述（≤300 字符）

> 一款关于折射光芒的霓虹 Roguelite 生存射击游戏。武器自动开火，你只需走位、冲刺、构筑流派。把武器分为红、绿、蓝三色，将满级武器融合为烈阳、寒霜与虚空，再引爆一记横扫全屏的棱光爆发！

### 关于这款游戏

你是一缕有生命的光，正坠过一道垂死光谱的四重深渊。这里的一切，都想把你熄灭。

**PRISMFALL** 是一款节奏飞快、光彩夺目的“割草”射击游戏：武器自动开火，你在成百上千的几何敌人间穿梭，收集光子，让每一次升级都把流派推向更亮、更强、更离谱。十分钟，一个首领，再来一局。

**调和光芒，不只是叠数值**
- 每件武器与被动都有色相：**赤红**主暴力输出，**翠绿**主生命与范围，**蔚蓝**主攻速与节奏。
- 将两把不同色相的武器升至满级即可**融合**：**烈阳**灼烧并释放连锁闪电，**寒霜**冰冻并震碎敌人，**虚空**追踪、吸血并撕开奇点。
- 同时持有三种融合武器，达成**棱光共鸣**——纯白之光，全效齐发。

**随心构筑**
- **15 种武器**——新月斩、环绕刃、抛射迫击炮、穿透激光、藤鞭、炮台、无人机、回旋棱镜……
- **15 种被动**，以及奖励专精单一色相的**共鸣**加成。
- **棱光爆发**：击杀积攒超载，释放一道依主色相染色的全屏冲击——重创、治疗或冻结一切。
- 带无敌帧的**冲刺**——有时候，最好的出路就是正面穿过去。

**坠向更深处**
- **4 个精心设计的关卡**——星云浅滩、赤红荒原、翠绿晶格与奇点，各有专属敌人、事件和音乐。
- **4 位多阶段首领**，从六角守卫一路打到光谱尽头的虚无棱镜。
- **6 名角色**，各有专属起始武器和玩法。
- 每个关卡都有**飞升**难度，还有为勇者准备的**无尽模式**。
- 在**棱镜熔炉**中花费流明永久强化，解锁角色、武器与成就。

**手感至上**
- 数千个发光图形流畅运行，HDR 泛光、命中停顿与屏幕震动（均可调节）。
- 全程实时合成、随战况升腾的动态配乐。
- **完整手柄支持**，适配 Steam Deck；键鼠操作可选手动瞄准。
- 辅助选项：减少闪烁、震屏强度、伤害数字、渲染比例。
- 支持英文与简体中文。
